About the company

Learning the ground before making claims about it.

Phoenician Gold Metals & Minerals LLC is a member-managed Arizona limited liability company based in the Phoenix area. We focus on metals, crystals, fossils, and other natural things of value — approached with research-first care and stewardship.

A measured beginning

We are taking a measured, research-first approach and beginning with Phase 1 work. The purpose is to build understanding through public information, observation, and practical questions—before considering any next step.

Our posture is intentionally modest: casual-use / hand-tool activity where appropriate, careful documentation, and attention to access, permissions, safety, land status, and applicable rules. Enjoyment of the land is part of how we work.

What we look for

Metals, crystals, fossils, and other natural things of value. We stay placer-minded and low-impact: curiosity and patience over industrial swagger. We leave the land’s beauty intact.

How the company is identified

The company is identified by its legal name, Arizona base, and mission. This site does not publish a roster of members, personal biographies, individual roles, photographs of faces, or private contact details. Inquiries go to the company mailbox.

Company-status note

The LLC name is available; formation is in progress. This draft does not add formation dates, ownership details, operating history, discoveries, production, or project inventory that has not been separately verified.

In plain terms: we are researching and evaluating, not presenting a proven deposit, completed claim strategy, or promised outcome.
