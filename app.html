What it does

Everything a day in the field needs.

Built for hobbyists who like to know where they are, what they found, and whether they’re welcome there. Every tool below is in the free account.

01

Tag finds with GPS and photos

One tap records your position, accuracy and time, plus photos and notes about what you found. Your log of finds builds itself as you walk.

02

Keep a target list

Save the spots you want to visit or revisit, sorted by distance, with your own notes on each. Plan the next trip from the couch.

03

Map with land status

USGS topo, imagery and street basemaps, starter places that welcome collectors, and a land check that shows who manages a point and whether casual collecting looks open.

04

Works offline

Download map areas before you head out, then tag finds and browse the map with no signal. Your data lives on the phone, so dead zones don’t stop you.

05

Private by design

Your finds, photos and targets are stored on your device in an encrypted vault, locked with your own PIN, with automatic lock. We don’t upload them.

06

Store and About

Browse the current shelf from our store and read about the company right inside the app.

Field App Pro

Go deeper when you’re ready.

Pro adds claim-research tools for serious collectors and small outfits: BLM surface management and mining-claim checks, a search outward for the nearest land that is likely open, call scripts for land managers, and KML exports for your other mapping tools.

Start with a free 3-day trial, then $99.99 a month, renewing monthly. Cancel anytime: your phone returns to the free account and your Pro data stays on it.

Get started

Up and running in a minute.

  1. Open the app. Visit app.phoeniciangoldmetals.com on your phone.
  2. Add it to your Home Screen. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen, then open it from there. On Android: the browser menu → Install app.
  3. Set a device PIN. It locks the app and encrypts what you save on this phone.
  4. Create your free account. Pick a username and an account PIN. Use them to sign in on any device.
