Pro · HD and 3D maps

Read the terrain from your phone.

HD and 3D maps are part of Pro. HD Satellite uses USDA NAIP aerial photography, sharp to zoom 19 (about 30–60 cm per pixel). HD Terrain lays USGS 1 m lidar shading over the topo map, so washes, benches and old workings stand out where a regular topo stays flat. HD Satellite + Lidar blends that lidar shading over the aerial photos, with a slider for how strong it is, so you see the ground and its shape at once. Switch to 3D on the Map tab or in Navigate to tilt and turn the ground in front of you.

The photos and the lidar line up to within a few meters, but they are often from different years, so a new road or washout can show in one and not the other. Where 1 m lidar hasn’t been flown, the app says so and the shading comes from coarser elevation data.

The free General account uses the standard USGS Topo, USGS Imagery + Topo and OpenStreetMap layers, and you can save USGS areas for offline use.

Work with no signal

With Pro, download an area with the HD layers and 3D terrain while you have signal. Out in the field, the maps, 3D view and GPS keep working.

Good to know: for 3D with no signal, download the area first with “3D terrain” ticked; anywhere else the map drops back to 2D until you reconnect. Pins, spots and claim search show in 2D, and 3D hill shapes are approximate up close. If Pro ends, the map switches back to a standard layer; HD areas you saved stay on your device for when Pro is back.

In every account

Everything a day in the field needs.

The free General account is a complete hobby kit. Everything below is included.

01

Tag finds with GPS and photos

One tap records your position, accuracy and time, plus photos and notes. Works with no signal. Your spots list builds itself as you go, with Jump to on map, Navigate and PDF or KML export.

02

Navigate to any spot

A live “you are here” map with your heading, GPS accuracy circle and a line to your target, plus a compass arrow and distance. It works offline, because GPS needs no data.

03

Verified places to dig

A hand-checked guide to free public collecting areas and paid dig sites, with what you can find, access notes, site rules and tips. Paid digs show the price and booking link. A plain-English rules screen covers where collecting is allowed.

04

Land checks you can trust

A research aid that checks public land records for your spot and its surroundings and flags closed or uncertain land. If a source can’t be reached or an answer is unclear, the spot is marked unverified, never open. Each check has a downloadable PDF report.

05

Private land warnings

Private and trust land at your spot counts as closed. Private parcels nearby show as red outlines on the map and in Navigate, with your live distance to the boundary and an alert when you get close.

06

Private by design

Sign in with a username and PIN. What you save stays on your device in an encrypted vault, locked with your own PIN, with automatic lock. We don’t upload it. Shop the Store right from the app, too.

Field App Pro

Go deeper when you’re ready.

Pro turns the app into a premium claim research suite, and everything you save with it stays on your device. See the ground in HD and 3D, search for the nearest land that looks open, look up mining claims at any pin, save promising parcels as targets, and keep your research in encrypted Libraries and a searchable Archive. Upgrading is seamless: everything you’ve saved stays right where it is.

Nearest open land searches up to 15 miles out. Each quarter-quarter box is scored over its whole area, not just its center, and every result gets the full land check (private and trust land, a 250 m buffer, claims by map and legal description, withdrawals, Federal Register notices and land sales) before it shows as open. Results appear one by one as each passes the full check, so the first answers show up sooner, with no checks skipped. Distances are straight-line to the box edge; road access isn’t checked. It’s a research aid, not a guarantee that land is open.

Start with a free 3-day trial, then $99.99 a month, renewing monthly, on one device at a time. Cancel anytime: your device returns to the free General account and your Pro data stays on it.

Get started

Up and running in a minute.

  1. Open the app. Visit app.phoeniciangoldmetals.com on your phone.
  2. Add it to your Home Screen. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen, then open it from there. On Android: the browser menu → Install app.
  3. Set a device PIN. It locks the app and encrypts what you save on this phone.
  4. Create your free account. Pick a username and an account PIN. Use them to sign in on any device.
