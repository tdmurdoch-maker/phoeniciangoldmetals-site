How this shelf works

Leave the land intact.

We sell only what we can part with from careful collecting, or from natural materials we find or source ourselves. Field work stays casual-use and hand-tool. We leave the beauty of the land as we found it.

Nothing offered here is guaranteed gold, a rarity claim, an assay, or a financial return. A crystal is quartz unless we say otherwise with evidence. A fossil is a study piece. A made item is a made item.

This page does not list member names, phone numbers, or a street address. Questions go to the company mailbox. Shipping and payment details, once checkout exists, come from Stripe — not from a personal note.
